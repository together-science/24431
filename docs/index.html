Who we are

Built by students, run by students.

Team 24431 competes in the FIRST Tech Challenge, an international robotics competition where teams of students design, build, program, and drive a robot to complete a new game challenge every season.

Every part of the build — chassis, drivetrain, mechanisms, wiring, and code — is designed and executed by our team members, coached and supported by mentors and volunteers who keep the workshop running.

  • 1 Design and machine our own chassis, drivetrain, and game mechanisms
  • 2 Program autonomous and driver-controlled robot behavior
  • 3 Compete at regional qualifiers and championship events across Arizona
  • 4 Practice Gracious Professionalism and Coopertition on and off the field
